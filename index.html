Welcome to my homepage!

Ce Lyu 吕策

I am a master’s student at East China Normal University, where I have been studying since 2024. Previously, I received my bachelor’s degree from the School of Computer Science and Technology at Shandong University in 2024.

My research focuses on database system reliability and automated testing, including logic bug detection, differential and metamorphic testing, and LLM-assisted test generation.
